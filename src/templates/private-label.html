Buyer FAQ

Questions buyers ask before ordering

What is the MOQ for custom socks?

For standard programmes, the typical MOQ starts from 500 pairs per style, colour and size. It can change with yarn, construction, logo method and packaging.

How long does custom sock sampling take?

Typical sampling takes one week when the product brief and artwork are clear. Complex construction or material sourcing can require more time.

How long is bulk production?

Typical bulk-production lead time is one month after sample and order confirmation. The schedule is confirmed for each order.

Can Huakui support branding, packaging and export?

Yes. Branding can include embroidery, jacquard and printing, plus private labels and retail packaging. Huakui can also assist with export documents and shipment coordination to the destination port. Final scope is confirmed for the order.
